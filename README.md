# ARaL

## Summary

* ARaL requires an estimated total capital ranging from **P1,500,000 (Minimum)** to **P2,500,000 (Maximum)** for initial development, validation, and early startup operations.


* The project operates with an estimated monthly burn rate of **P208,000**.



---

## Problem

* Public-sector procurement cycles are slower and budget-locked, and therefore should not be assumed as early revenue sources.



---

## Users

* **Primary Paying Customers:** Private senior high schools, colleges and universities, technical-vocational institutions, training centres, and corporate technical training departments.


* **Second-Phase Channels (Public Sector):** DepEd divisions, TESDA scholarship programmes, local government units, and DICT skills initiatives.


* **Sponsored Seat Beneficiaries:** Public senior high school and TESDA scholarship learners reached through the Sponsored Seat Programme.



---

## Features & Scope

* **Skills Packs:** Production of 3 to 5 Skills Packs utilizing contracted 3D artists and asset licences.


* **Expert Validation:** 8 to 12 subject-matter expert reviews and competency mapping.


* **Pilot Deployments:** Partner-institution travel, sessions, materials, and participant incentives across 3 to 6 pilots.


* **Sponsored Seat Programme:** Delivery of 1 sponsored seat at no cost to a public senior high school or TESDA scholarship learner for every 10 paid institutional seats, funded from gross margin rather than grants.


* **Market Development:** Demonstrations, sales collateral, conferences, and early customer acquisition.


* **Compliance & Legal:** SEC/DTI registration, trademark filing, data privacy compliance, and accounting.



---

## Stack & Tooling

* **Development Infrastructure:** Hosting, CI, AR SDK, design, and development tools allocated for 12 months.


* **Test Devices:** 6 to 10 test handsets spanning all three delivery tiers.



---

## Team

* **Core Team:** 4 team members with budgeted monthly stipends ranging from P15,000 to P22,000 over a 12-month period.



---
